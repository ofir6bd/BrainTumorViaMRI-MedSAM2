How the fine-tune works

    
    
      
  1. Every slice becomes a colour picture — red = T1C, green = T2, blue = FLAIR — and the expert whole-tumour mask is the answer. Slices with no tumour are kept: they teach YOLO when to draw nothing.
    2. 
      
  2. Start from a YOLO that can already see (pre-trained on everyday photos) and teach it only the new job.
    3. 
      
  3. Guess, compare, correct — in groups of  slices; one round (epoch) = every training slice once.
    4. 
      
  4. Keep the best round — checked on yolo_val after every round (select.by: ); stops after  rounds without gain, at most .
    5. 
      
  5. Final score on whole patients: 3D Dice per patient, with the mirrored slice averaged in (tta_flip) and specks under  voxels removed, at confidence .

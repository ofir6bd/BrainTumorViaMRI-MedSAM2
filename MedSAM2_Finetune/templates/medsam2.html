How this fine-tune works

    
    
      
  1. YOLO looks first. Each brain slice (red = T1C, green = T2, blue = FLAIR) goes through a fine-tuned YOLO; we keep its probability for every pixel.
    2. 
      
  2. Every slice gets YOLO's hint as a mask prompt — MedSAM2's job is to fix that guess, not to find the tumour from scratch.
    3. 
      
  3. The neighbours are in memory. The start slice (YOLO's most confident) is segmented alone; two passes then run up and down the stack, each slice seeing its hint and the memory of the slices just passed.
    4. 
      
  4. Training teaches fixing, not copying:  of training hints are damaged on purpose while the answer stays the expert mask.
    5. 
      
  5. When running: the mirrored patient is averaged in and pieces under  voxels are removed.
    6. 
      
  6. The expert mask is only ever the answer sheet — never shown to the model as a prompt, never used to pick slices.
